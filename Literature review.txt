📚 Literature Review
A literature review is an important part of a research proposal that examines existing studies and information related to the chosen topic. 🔍 It helps the researcher understand what has already been studied and identifies gaps, limitations, and unanswered questions in previous research. 📖 It may include information from research papers 📝, academic journals 📰, books 📚, reports 📄, and reliable online sources 🌐. By comparing and analyzing previous studies, the researcher can develop a strong foundation for the current research. 💡 A well-written literature review also helps support the research problem, objectives, and methodology. 🎯 Overall, it provides a clear understanding of existing knowledge and shows how the proposed research can contribute something new. ⭐
