📅 Research Timeline
A research timeline is a planned schedule that shows the different activities and stages of a research project from beginning to end. 📝 It helps the researcher organize the work and complete each task within the required time. ⏰ A typical research timeline includes selecting the research topic 🎯, reviewing the literature 📚, collecting data 📊, analyzing the data 🔍, preparing the results 📈, writing the research report 📝, and presenting the findings 🎤. It also helps in setting deadlines, managing time effectively, and avoiding unnecessary delays. ✅ A well-prepared research timeline makes the research process systematic, organized, and efficient 🚀 and increases the chances of completing the research successfully within the given period. 🎓
