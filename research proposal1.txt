📚 Research Proposal
A research proposal is a detailed plan that describes a proposed research study. 🔍 It explains what the researcher wants to study, why the topic is important, and how the research will be carried out.

🎯 It includes the research objectives and research questions that guide the study.
📖 A literature review presents previous studies and helps identify gaps in existing knowledge.
🧪 The research methodology explains the methods used to collect and analyze data.
⭐ The significance of the study describes how the research can contribute to knowledge or society.
📅 A timeline shows the different stages of the research process.
📑 Finally, references provide the sources used to support the research.

✨ Overall, a research proposal provides a clear roadmap for conducting meaningful and systematic research.
