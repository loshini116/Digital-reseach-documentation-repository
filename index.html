📖 Literature Review

A literature review is a systematic study of existing research, academic papers, books, journals and other scholarly sources related to a research topic.

Purpose

  • Understand existing research and theories.
  • Identify important findings from previous studies.
  • Compare different approaches and methodologies.
  • Identify gaps in existing research.
  • Provide a foundation for the proposed research.

Existing Research

Review previous studies and established knowledge.

Research Gap

Identify problems that have not been adequately addressed.

Critical Analysis

Compare findings, methods and conclusions.

Research Direction

Use previous knowledge to guide the new study.

📝 Research Proposal

Title

Development of a Digital Research Documentation Repository

Problem Statement

Researchers often manage academic documents using disconnected files and folders. This can make document organization, searching, sharing and tracking difficult.

Objectives

  • Develop a centralized research documentation platform.
  • Provide organized storage for research materials.
  • Enable quick searching and retrieval of documents.
  • Maintain references and research records.
  • Improve collaboration and documentation management.

Proposed Methodology

The project will follow a structured research and development process involving literature review, requirement collection, system design, implementation, testing and evaluation.
