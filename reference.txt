📖 References
References are an essential part of a research proposal because they provide information about the sources used to support the research. 🔍 They can include books 📚, academic journals 📰, research articles 📝, websites 🌐, reports 📄, and other reliable sources. References help the researcher build a strong foundation for the study and show that the research is based on existing knowledge and evidence. ⭐

✍️ Proper referencing gives credit to the original authors and prevents plagiarism. It also allows readers to verify the information and explore the topic further. 🔗 Using a consistent referencing style, such as APA, MLA, or Harvard, makes the research professional and easy to understand. ✅ Therefore, accurate and well-organized references improve the quality, reliability, and credibility of a research proposal. 🌟
